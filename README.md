# MaintenAI

> AI-Powered Industrial Maintenance & Technical Knowledge Management Platform

MaintenAI is an intelligent maintenance management platform designed to centralize machine information, technical documentation, fault records, and maintenance knowledge in a single system.

It combines traditional maintenance workflows with AI-assisted technical document search and question answering, helping technical teams access relevant information faster and preserve organizational knowledge.

## ✨ Key Features

### 🏭 Machine Management
Centralized machine and equipment records with structured technical information.

### 📚 Technical Document Management
Upload and organize manuals, maintenance documents, and machine-related technical files.

### 🔧 Fault Recording & Tracking
Create, classify, prioritize, and track equipment faults throughout their lifecycle.

### 🛠 Maintenance Records
Maintain structured maintenance histories for machines and equipment.

### 🤖 AI Technical Assistant
Ask natural-language questions about technical documentation and receive context-aware answers based on relevant sources.

### 🔎 Semantic Document Search
Search technical information based on meaning rather than relying only on exact keyword matches.

### 👥 Role & User Management
Manage users and access levels for different operational responsibilities.

### 📊 Operational Dashboard
Monitor machines, maintenance activities, faults, and system information from a centralized interface.

---

## 🧠 AI-Assisted Knowledge Retrieval

MaintenAI transforms technical documentation into an accessible knowledge source.

Instead of manually searching through large manuals and maintenance files, users can ask technical questions in natural language and receive answers based on the available documentation.

The system is designed to support faster troubleshooting while keeping the original technical sources accessible for verification.

## 🎯 Project Goal

Industrial maintenance knowledge is often scattered across technical documents, individual employees, spreadsheets, and disconnected systems.

MaintenAI aims to bring this information together and make it **searchable, traceable, and usable during real maintenance operations**.

The long-term goal is to create an intelligent maintenance platform capable of combining technical documentation, historical maintenance data, equipment information, and operational data to support better maintenance decisions.
